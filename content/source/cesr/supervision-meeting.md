# Initial meeting — additional agenda
- [ ] Confirm career history mapped to the 14 HiLLOs; identify placement gaps against the required list (General ICM, Anaesthesia, Medicine, Neuro, Cardiothoracic, Paediatric)
- [ ] Confirm examination status (FFICM/EDIC/DICM/FCICM) and plan if none held
- [ ] Agree SSY declaration area
- [ ] Check the seven-year currency of each historic placement and flag maintenance work needed
- [ ] Identify the three Structured Report authors
- [ ] Set the target submission window and meeting schedule
- [ ] Walk through this workspace: HiLLO pages, evidence logs, the Evidence rules page

<!-- subpage: "Mid-placement meeting additions" -->
# Mid-placement meeting — additional agenda
- [ ] Review WBA counts this placement against the targets on the relevant HiLLO page
- [ ] Confirm the placement logbook is up to date and export-ready
- [ ] Book remaining assessments and any required courses before the placement ends
- [ ] Capture level-of-care decisions and emergencies while they are fresh (reflections)

<!-- subpage: "End-of-placement meeting additions" -->
# End-of-placement meeting — additional agenda
- [ ] Supervisor report for this placement drafted and its coverage of each key capability checked
- [ ] Placement evidence filed against the relevant HiLLO pages with cross-references
- [ ] Rota samples and unit caseload data for this placement saved
- [ ] Outstanding gaps carried into the next placement's objectives

<!-- subpage: "Pre-submission meeting additions" -->
# Pre-submission meeting — additional agenda
- [ ] Every HiLLO at "Ready for review" or "Signed off" on the master table
- [ ] Cross-cutting checklist on the Evidence rules page fully ticked (structured reports, 20+ SLEs, 3 MSFs, 4 reflections, referrals, appraisals, rotas, CV)
- [ ] Anonymisation audit of every document
- [ ] Verifier pro formas signed
- [ ] Evidence grouped into combined PDFs matching the online application sequences
- [ ] Mock evaluation: supervisor challenges the weakest HiLLO
