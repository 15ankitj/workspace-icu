# 📋 Evidence rules that apply everywhere

> ℹ️ Read this page first and revisit it before submission. Every HiLLO page in this workspace assumes these rules; they are not repeated there.

## The application at a glance
- The standard assessed is the **Knowledge, Skills and Experience (KSE)** of a UK specialist, framed by the **14 HiLLOs** of the ICM CCT curriculum.
- Required placements: **General ICM (minimum 2¼ years), Anaesthesia, Medicine, Neuro ICM, Cardiothoracic ICM, Paediatric ICM.** An application without evidence of these placements and experiential learning is unlikely to succeed.
- You must also declare a **Special Skills Year (SSY)** area, evidenced at a **higher capability level** than the curriculum requires for that HiLLO.
- Most applications contain **800–1,000 pages**. Quality, relevance and organisation beat volume.
- You **cannot compensate** for weak evidence in one HiLLO with extra evidence in another — this is explicit for HiLLOs 10–14.

## Mandatory cross-cutting items
- [ ] **Three Structured Reports minimum** — one from your current Clinical Director, at least two more from colleagues practising ICM who have worked with you within the last two years
- [ ] **At least 20 SLEs** overall (in addition to HiLLO 10's own SLE requirement), balanced across HiLLOs
- [ ] **At least three MSFs**, from different times and placements (or contemporaneous colleague feedback if unavailable)
- [ ] **At least four reflective pieces**, spread across the application, mixing clinical and non-clinical practice
- [ ] **At least two referral letters/emails** (discharge summaries, coroner reports acceptable)
- [ ] **Appraisals from the last three years** of clinical practice
- [ ] **Sample rotas for every placement** (e.g. eight consecutive weeks for a 1:8 rota)
- [ ] **Departmental/unit annual caseload statistics** for placements in the last seven years
- [ ] **CV** in GMC format, matched by employment letters and job descriptions
- [ ] **Examination:** FFICM, or an accepted equivalent (EDIC, DICM, FCICM with full programme); without one, a full FFICM-mapping portfolio is required and success is at FICM's discretion — EDAIC is explicitly insufficient

## Currency — the seven-year rule
Evidence older than **seven years (whole-time equivalent)** does not demonstrate current capability on its own. You need not repeat the placement; you must evidence **maintenance** (logbooks, SLEs, reflections, referrals, ongoing practice). CPD alone is insufficient to demonstrate maintenance. Each specialty HiLLO page in this workspace has a maintenance checklist.

## Anonymisation (GMC will return non-compliant evidence)
Remove from every document before upload or submission:
- [ ] All patient names and relatives' names
- [ ] Addresses and contact details
- [ ] NHS numbers and any other patient numbers
- [ ] GMC numbers of colleagues you have assessed, referenced, or complained about
Gender and date of birth do **not** need anonymising for the GMC — but **WorkspaceICU must never hold patient-identifiable information in any form**, so anonymise fully before anything is attached here.

## Organising and cross-referencing
- Follow the online application structure: training/qualifications/CV sequences, then 14 HiLLO sequences, then the SSY sequence.
- **Never upload duplicates.** Provide one copy, list it under each relevant HiLLO, and state where it lives. An index document per HiLLO is valued by evaluators — the "My evidence" logs on each HiLLO page here generate exactly that.
- **Triangulate:** pair every claimed capability with more than one evidence type (e.g. SLE + logbook + reflection). Primary evidence of personal involvement outweighs unit-level data; caseload statistics support but never suffice alone.
- Group documents into combined PDFs to keep uploads manageable; everything must be legible and in English (certified translations where not).
- Verification: every piece of evidence needs the signed verifier pro forma; overseas qualifications must be authenticated.
